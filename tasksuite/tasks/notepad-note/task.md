Open Notepad and type this note exactly:

Museum visit notes

The natural history museum opens at 9:30 on weekdays.
The planetarium show costs 12 euros and runs at 11:00 and 14:00.
Remember: no flash photography in the gem hall.

Todo after the visit: buy the guidebook and email Anna the photos.

Save the note as "museum-notes.txt" inside "{{.ScratchDir}}" using
Notepad's Save As dialog, then close Notepad.
