---
title: Draw a line on the Paint canvas and save the drawing
category: canvas
tags: canvas, drag, sparse-tree, save-as
apps: mspaint
---

Open Paint (mspaint). Select the Pencil tool and draw one continuous
line across the canvas, starting near the top-left corner and ending
near the bottom-right corner.

Save the drawing as "line.png" inside "{{.ScratchDir}}" (a PNG image),
then close Paint.
