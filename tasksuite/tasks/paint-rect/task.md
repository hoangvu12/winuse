---
title: Draw a rectangle in Paint and save the drawing
category: canvas
tags: canvas, shapes, sparse-tree, save-as
apps: mspaint
---

Open Paint (mspaint). Using the rectangle shape tool, draw a rectangle
roughly a quarter of the canvas in size, in the middle of the canvas.

Save the drawing as "rect.png" inside "{{.ScratchDir}}" (a PNG image),
then close Paint.
